👥 Users

🎯 User-Level Budgets (ULB)

ULBs cap individual user consumption across both pool and metered phases. Always a hard stop. Precedence: Individual > Cost Center > Universal.

Universal ULB

Applies to all licensed users unless overridden

Cost Center ULBs

Per-user budget for members of each cost center. Overrides universal ULB.

Individual ULBs

Per-user budget for specific users. Overrides both cost center and universal ULBs.

💳 Overage Budgets

These budgets cap metered charges ($0.01/credit) once the pool is exhausted. Can be configured with or without hard stop.
